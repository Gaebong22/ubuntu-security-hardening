=== UDISKS2 HARDENING RESULT ===

[Before]
$ systemctl is-enabled udisks2.service
enabled

$ systemctl status udisks2.service --no-pager
● udisks2.service - Disk Manager
     Loaded: loaded (/usr/lib/systemd/system/udisks2.service; enabled; preset: enabled)
     Active: active (running)

[Reverse Dependency]
$ systemctl list-dependencies --reverse udisks2.service --no-pager
udisks2.service
● └─graphical.target

[Display Manager]
$ systemctl is-active display-manager.service
inactive

[Action]
$ sudo systemctl disable --now udisks2.service
Removed '/etc/systemd/system/graphical.target.wants/udisks2.service'.

[After]
$ systemctl is-enabled udisks2.service
disabled

$ systemctl is-active udisks2.service
inactive

[Reboot Follow-up]
$ systemctl is-active ModemManager.service multipathd.service udisks2.service
inactive
inactive
active

$ systemctl status udisks2.service --no-pager
● udisks2.service - Disk Manager
     Loaded: loaded (/usr/lib/systemd/system/udisks2.service; disabled; preset: enabled)
     Active: active (running)

udisks2 was activated on demand after reboot even though the unit was disabled.
